## /help
envoi : <b>Commands</b>

📊 <b>Status</b>
/status — global status
/down — ongoing alerts (with their number)
/digest — digest now
/refresh — run all checks again

🔕 <b>Alerts</b>
/ack &lt;number|key&gt; &lt;reason&gt; — acknowledge (stops reminders)
/mute &lt;target&gt; &lt;duration&gt; &lt;reason&gt; — silence, e.g. <code>/mute web-1 2h migration</code>
<i>target: alert number (#12), host, service, rule or key; duration: 30m, 2h, 3d (30 d at most)</i>
/unmute &lt;number&gt; — lift a silence
/silences — active silences

🌐 <b>Network</b>
/network — LAN devices, new ones, MACs to confirm (to delete a device: on /network, with a reason)
/device &lt;text&gt; — find a device (name, MAC, vendor, exact IP)
/known &lt;id&gt; &lt;reason&gt; — confirm a new device or one to review

<i>Old names still accepted: /reseau, /appareil, /connu</i>
